# Book a conference room

Rooms are booked from the calendar; a few have special equipment.

## Steps

1. Add the room as an attendee in the calendar invite.
2. `Osprey` seats 8 on the 4th floor and has the video conferencing unit; `Kestrel` seats 4 on the 2nd floor.
3. Bookings during 09:00-17:00 are limited to 2 hours.

## Gotchas

Recurring bookings are released automatically if the room is empty 15 minutes after the start time.
