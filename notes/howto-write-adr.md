# Write an architecture decision record

ADRs capture decisions with lasting impact.

## Steps

1. Copy `docs/adr/0000-template.md` to the next sequential number.
2. Fill in Context, Decision, Alternatives considered, and Consequences; the alternatives section is mandatory.
3. Open a PR; the ADR moves from `Proposed` to `Accepted` when two senior engineers approve.

## Gotchas

Superseded ADRs are never deleted; add a `Superseded by` line instead.
